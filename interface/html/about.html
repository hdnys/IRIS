What is IRIS?

IRIS is an Intelligent Resource for Short-Sighted Individuals. It's an advanced computer vision system designed to assist people with visual deficiencies by providing real-time scene understanding and object detection capabilities.

Our Mission

Our mission is to empower individuals with visual impairments by providing accessible technology that enhances their awareness of their surroundings and helps them navigate the world with greater independence and confidence.

Key Features

  • Real-time Scene Understanding: Advanced computer vision analyzes your surroundings in real-time
  • Object Detection: Identifies and describes objects, people, and obstacles around you
  • Face Recognition: Recognizes people from your entourage and identifies them by name
  • Voice Control: Control the system entirely through voice commands in French or English
  • Visual Profile Adaptation: Customizable settings for different types of visual deficiencies
  • Personalized Entourage: Learn and recognize people important to you

Voice Commands

IRIS features a comprehensive voice control system that allows you to operate the interface without visual interaction. You can:

  • Start and stop the pipeline with voice commands
  • Add new people to your entourage
  • Access settings and preferences
  • Modify your visual deficiency profile
  • Request audio descriptions of pages and content

Technology Stack

IRIS is built using state-of-the-art deep learning models and computer vision techniques:

  • YOLOv8 for real-time object detection
  • SFace for advanced face recognition
  • MobileNetV2 for image classification
  • Web Speech API for voice control
  • FastAPI backend for high-performance processing

Privacy & Security

Your privacy is important to us. All face recognition data is stored locally on your device or server. No data is sent to external servers without your consent.
